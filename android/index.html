DESIGNED AROUND YOUR PRACTICE

Every repetition has its place.

Simple tools to begin, keep going and look back.

01

Count with intention

Choose 27, 54, 108, 1008 or your own goal. Tap to count, pause when you need to, and undo an accidental tap.

02

Make it your mantra

Explore 32 presets across five categories, save favorites or enter your own text. Android counting is manual, without microphone or voice recognition.

03

Come back tomorrow

Set a daily sankalp. See your streak, seven-day progress and saved sessions. Try a seven-day challenge at your own pace.

04

Keep your progress

Export and merge a local JSON history backup through the Android document picker. Automatic cloud sync is not included.

05

Feel your rhythm

Optional haptics, volume-key counting and a soft completion chime. Reduce motion and keep-awake controls are available.

06

Share a small milestone

Create a practice, streak or challenge card and choose where to share it. No public leaderboard or account is required.

A QUIETER AD EXPERIENCE

Practice first.

The free Android edition is supported by advertising. A banner can appear on Progress, and an occasional frequency-limited ad may appear after a qualifying completed session. Ads do not interrupt active counting.

YOUR CHOICES MATTER

Stay in control.

Practice history stays on your phone unless you export or share it. Notification permission is requested on first launch on Android 13+. Remote updates can be disabled in Settings; daily reminders are separate.

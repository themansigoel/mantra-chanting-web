A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Simple, from the start.

Choose an edition to see its complete feature details.

Is the Android app available on Google Play?

Not publicly yet. The Android edition is in testing. We will add the public Google Play link here when it launches.

Are the Android and iOS features the same?

Each edition has its own features. Android currently focuses on manual counting, six visual styles and local practice history. Apple Watch and speech features belong to the Apple edition.

Do I need an account on Android?

No account is required. Core counting and practice history work offline. Ads, remote notifications and analytics use internet services; see our platform-specific privacy information.

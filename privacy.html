Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Mansi Agarwal ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Mantra Chanting: Japa Mala mobile application for iOS and watchOS. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. Our software is built from the ground up to respect your personal boundaries and explain how practice features and third-party services handle information.

1. Local progress and third-party services

We do not operate external web servers, databases, or cloud accounts to log your meditation records. All information you configure in the app — including custom focus mantras, session targets, running streaks, and historical completion logs — is saved locally on your device or in your personal iCloud container. We have no access to this data and cannot compile, share, or sell it.

2. On-Device Acoustic Analysis Processing

To enable hands-free acoustic counting:

  • Microphone Input: The app utilizes the device microphone to detect sound peak classifications. This audio stream is processed instantly in the device's volatile memory and is never saved or exported.
  • Speech Recognition: Speech recognition uses Apple speech services with your permission. Processing availability depends on the device, language and recognition service; do not assume every recognition request stays on-device.

3. StoreKit 2.0 & Apple Purchases

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Apple's native App Store checkout interface. No credit card information, billing details, or user identity parameters are shared with us.

4. Google Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging

The app initializes Firebase Analytics on launch and sends app usage events such as session activity, selected modes and feature interactions, together with technical app/device information. Firebase can use app-instance identifiers; this data should not be described as guaranteed anonymous. Notification permission is separate. See Google’s Firebase privacy information for provider processing and controls.

5. Upcoming Android edition: ads and analytics

The Android edition is coming soon and is not yet publicly available. The Apple-specific microphone, speech, StoreKit and iCloud descriptions above apply to iOS/watchOS. The initial Android edition uses manual counting and local practice storage, with user-initiated JSON backup export/import; automatic cloud backup is not enabled.

The free Android edition will use Google AdMob for a Progress-screen banner and occasional interstitial ads after qualifying completed practices. Ads are kept outside active chanting. Ad services may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics, depending on configuration, regional requirements and consent. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) will handle applicable consent and privacy choices.

Firebase Analytics is planned for app usage and technical events. Android collection follows the applicable analytics-storage consent or consent-not-required status; unknown or denied consent and previous explicit declines keep collection off. There is no separate app analytics switch. Custom mantra text and religious selections are excluded from analytics events.

Android advertising and analytics account configuration and release disclosures are still being finalized. This section describes the planned release behavior and will be reviewed before launch. Provider details: Google privacy policy and Firebase privacy.

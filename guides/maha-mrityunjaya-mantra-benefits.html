Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra: 108 Chanting Rules & Healing Benefits

Updated August 30, 2026 • 5 min read

The Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra (the Great Death-Conquering Mantra from the Rigveda) is dedicated to Tryambaka (Lord Shiva). Practiced for millennia, this mantra releases deep subconscious fears, supports physical recovery, and fosters emotional resilience.

ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान् मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात् ॥

Meaning:

"We meditate upon the Three-Eyed Lord Shiva, who is fragrant and nourishes all beings. Just as a ripe cucumber is naturally severed from its vine, may He liberate us from death and mortal attachment for the sake of immortality."

Gayatri Mantra: 108 Times Chanting Meaning, Lyrics & Spiritual Rules

Updated August 30, 2026 • 6 min read

The Gayatri Mantra is widely revered as the Supreme Mother of the Vedas (Veda Mata). Chanting this sacred hymn 108 times daily awakens inner solar wisdom, purifies mental stress, and establishes divine tranquility in the practitioner's consciousness.

ॐ भूर् भुवः स्वः ।
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं ।
भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि ।
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥

English Transliteration:

Om Bhur Bhuvaḥ Svaḥ
Tat-savitur Vareṇyaṃ
Bhargo Devasya Dhīmahi
Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Pracodayāt

Word-by-Word English Meaning:

  • Om: The primordial primordial cosmic sound.
  • Bhur Bhuvaḥ Svaḥ: The physical realm, the mental astral realm, and the celestial spiritual realm.
  • Tat Savitur: That radiant divine solar illumination.
  • Vareṇyaṃ: Most adorable, supreme, and worthy of adoration.
  • Bhargo: Spiritual effulgence that destroys darkness and ignorance.
  • Devasya: Divine Grace.
  • Dhīmahi: We meditate upon.
  • Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Pracodayāt: May that divine light illuminate our intellect and direct our righteous path.

Why Chant 108 Times?

In Vedic astrology and sacred geometry, the distance between the Earth and the Sun is roughly 108 times the Sun's diameter. Completing a 108-bead Japa Mala aligns individual biological rhythms with cosmic planetary order, creating profound mental clarity.
